Website privacy

This page explains how data is handled when you visit the Clinkwork website or contact support.

This website

No analytics, advertising pixels or external fonts are used here. GitHub Pages, the intended hosting provider, may process technical request data, including IP addresses, to provide and secure the site. This is separate from the apps' Data Not Collected label.

GitHub privacy statement

Your work stays with you

Settings and, where available, saved creations are stored on your device. Clear the site's local storage to remove browser settings. Shared machines are carried in a link's fragment; Clinkwork does not upload them to a server.

Purposes and legal bases

Necessary local settings support the service you request. Hosting and security are intended to rely on legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); support on pre-contractual or contractual steps where applicable (Art. 6(1)(b)), otherwise legitimate interests.

Contact

If you contact support, the information you send is used to answer your request. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Your rights and retention

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability and object to processing. You may complain to a supervisory authority.

If you contact support, we use the details in your message only to handle your request and keep them only as long as that and statutory retention duties require. Settings stored in your browser stay there until you delete them. GitHub, as the hosting provider, determines how long technical access data is kept. To exercise your rights, contact us at the address above.
