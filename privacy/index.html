Clinkwork / Privacy policy

Data Not Collected

No analytics. No accounts. Clinkwork does not collect personal data through Loop Machine or Forge. Loop codes stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Your work stays with you

Settings and, where available, saved creations are stored on your device. Clear the site's local storage to remove browser settings. Shared machines are carried in a link's fragment; Clinkwork does not upload them to a server.

Apple services

In the iOS editions, purchases are handled by Apple. If you choose to use Game Center, Apple's services process the information needed for that feature under Apple's privacy policy.

This website

No analytics, advertising pixels or external fonts are used here. GitHub Pages, the intended hosting provider, may process technical request data, including IP addresses, to provide and secure the site. This is separate from the apps' Data Not Collected label.

Website privacy

Contact

If you contact support, the information you send is used to answer your request. Do not send passwords or payment details.
