Support

A little help, back to making.

Start here if a toy needs a nudge.

No sound?

Tap inside the toy to enable audio, check the sound setting, then check your device volume.

The toy will not load?

Use a recent browser with WebGL2 enabled. Close other graphics-heavy tabs and try again. The web editions need JavaScript; the rest of this site works without it.

Where are my creations?

Creations are local where saving is available. There is no account or cloud backup. Keep a copy of a shared machine link before clearing browser data.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Your purchases are tied to your Apple Account.

Can I share my unlocks with my family?

Yes! All unlocks can be shared through Apple Family Sharing.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles refunds. You can request one here: reportaproblem.apple.com

Are there any subscriptions?

No subscriptions. Every unlock is a one-time purchase.

Where are my creations on iPhone?

Your creations are stored on your iPhone. There is no cloud backup or Clinkwork account.
